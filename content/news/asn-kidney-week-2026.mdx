The American Society of Nephrology (ASN) holds **Kidney Week 2026** in person at the Colorado Convention Center in **Denver, October 21-25**. It remains the field's largest gathering for clinicians, researchers, and trainees.

Early Programs run **Wednesday, October 21**, followed by the main scientific sessions **Thursday through Sunday**. This year's Early Programs add a new **Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic (CKM) Health** track alongside established offerings in glomerular diseases, critical care nephrology, onconephrology, ADPKD, and point-of-care ultrasound.

There is **no virtual or hybrid option** in 2026. All content will be posted to the ASN Learning Center in mid-November for those who cannot attend.
