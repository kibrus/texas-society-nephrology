date: "2026-09-25"

category: "Treatment"
---

The FDA has expanded the label for **finerenone (Kerendia)** to include adults with **chronic kidney disease associated with type 1 diabetes**, making it the only non-steroidal mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist (MRA) indicated for CKD across both type 1 and type 2 diabetes.

In the supporting trial, albuminuria fell early and durably, with placebo-adjusted reductions in urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio of roughly **22% at three months and 28% at six months**, a marker associated with slower kidney disease progression.

The expansion gives nephrologists another disease-modifying option for proteinuric diabetic kidney disease, a population that has historically had limited targeted therapy.
