Focal segmental glomerulosclerosis (FSGS) has long been treated with supportive care and off-label immunosuppression. In 2026, **sparsentan** became the **first therapy with full FDA approval specifically for FSGS**, a significant shift in how the disease can be managed.

Sparsentan is a single molecule that blocks **both the endothelin type A receptor and the angiotensin II type 1 receptor**, two pathways that drive glomerular injury and proteinuria. Its approval was supported by the **DUPLEX and DUET** trials, which showed meaningful reductions in proteinuria.

The approval is especially timely for our community: Dr. Gerald Appel of Columbia University will cover these advances in his upcoming **Bayou City Bean Club** talk, *"The Progress in FSGS."*
