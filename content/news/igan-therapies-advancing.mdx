IgA nephropathy (IgAN) is seeing one of the most active treatment pipelines in nephrology, as the field moves from blood-pressure and proteinuria control toward **disease-modifying therapy**.

Among the developments reported in 2026:

- **Atacicept** — which targets the BAFF and APRIL pathways involved in pathogenic IgA production — is under **FDA priority review** for IgAN.
- **Iptacopan**, a complement (factor B) inhibitor, showed **slower eGFR decline and fewer kidney-failure events** in Phase 3 data.
- Additional agents targeting the same immune pathways continue to report **marked reductions in proteinuria**.

Together these signal a near-term expansion of options for a disease that often progresses to kidney failure in younger adults.
