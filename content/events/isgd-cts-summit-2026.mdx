The International Society of Glomerular Disease (ISGD) and the University of Texas Medical Branch (UTMB) are excited to invite you to the inaugural **ISGD Clinical Trials Summit**, to be held November 13-15, 2026, at the Galveston Island Convention Center.

**Where:** Galveston Island Convention Center

The goal for this inaugural summit is to bring every stakeholder in the kidney trial ecosystem together in one room: clinical trialists and investigators, patients and patient advocacy organizations, study coordinators and trainees, regulators, pharmaceutical and biotechnology leaders, venture capitalists and AI experts.

The program was shaped by an international Program Advisory Committee and features innovative sessions and ample opportunities for networking as well as a rich social program.

**Program highlights include:**

- Keynote address on how AI is reshaping the pace of clinical trials
- "Voice of the FDA" roundtable with several CDER/CBER nephrologists
- Sessions on emerging biomarkers and treatment targets in glomerular disease
- Dedicated sessions on pediatric and post-transplant recurrent disease trials
- Roundtables featuring venture capital investors and pharmaceutical C-suite executives
- Late-breaking research including new trial data that will not be presented at ASN Kidney Week

Registration details are available at [is-gd.org/2026-cts](https://www.is-gd.org/2026-cts). In addition to all sessions/meals at the convention center, registration also includes entry to the poolside welcome reception and dinner at the San Luis Resort on Friday, November 13.

We hope you can join us in welcoming our global colleagues to Texas!
