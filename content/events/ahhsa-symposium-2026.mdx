Join the Center for Precision Medicine at UT Health San Antonio for the **2nd Annual Advances in Human Health through Spatial Omics and AI Symposium (AHHSA 2026)** - two days of multidisciplinary breakthrough science, systems medicine, and hands-on workshops. This year's theme is **“Entering the Multi-Ome.”**

**When:** October 5-6, 2026
**Where:** UT Health San Antonio · San Antonio, Texas

## Keynote Speaker

**Julia Guthrie, PhD** - Ludwig Boltzmann Institute for Network Medicine, Vienna, Austria. An expert in the protein interactome, Dr. Guthrie will present *“From Omics to Insight: Interactive Network Medicine for Multi-Omics Discovery,”* featuring a new Virtual Reality tool to enhance data integration for clinical applications.

## Workshops & Tutorials

- Virtual Reality-Network Biology → Pathways
- Organoids and point-of-care testing

## Abstract Topics

Systems biology · AI · Omics & spatial omics · Organoids · Digital pathology · Immunotherapy · Translational science · Clinical trials and therapeutics

## Travel Awards

Travel awards are available for trainees and junior faculty. Submit your abstract to apply. Abstract submission deadline is **August 28, 2026**.
